This website, flareonix.in, and the Flareonix community, programmes and events (including the **Flareonix Founders Table**) are run by **Flareonix**, owned and operated by **Aayush Manjhariya** (Founder & CEO), based in Kashipur, Uttarakhand, India ("Flareonix", "we", "us").

For personal data collected through our website, forms and events, Flareonix decides why and how the data is used. Under Indian data protection law, that makes us the **Data Fiduciary**.

**Contact for privacy questions:** [hello@flareonix.in](mailto:hello@flareonix.in)

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the rights you have. It applies when you:

- visit or browse our website;
- sign in with Google;
- fill in a form (contact, growth consultation, founder or freelancer interest);
- use our AI Tools;
- comment on blog posts or events;
- register for, attend or apply to speak or pitch at an event;
- contact us by email, WhatsApp or social media.

## 3. Personal data we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account details | Name, email address, profile photo | Google sign-in |
| Form submissions | Name, email, WhatsApp number, city, message, business details | You |
| Event registration (when open) | Name, email, phone, pass type, payment reference (for example a UPI transaction ID), check-in status | You and our payment provider |
| Pitch Arena applications (when open) | Founder details, startup details, stage, pitch deck | You |
| Comments | Name, profile photo, comment text | You |
| AI Tools | The prompts you type and the results generated | You |

**We never ask for or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking password.** Payments are handled by the payment provider or bank you choose.

## 4. Why we use your data

We use personal data only for specific purposes:

1. **To provide what you ask for:** sign-in, replying to enquiries, consultations, AI Tools, comments, event passes, entry and check-in.
2. **To run events safely:** verifying passes, preventing duplicate or fraudulent entry, managing capacity and keeping attendees safe.
3. **To review applications:** Pitch Arena, speaker, sponsor, stall, ambassador and partner applications.
4. **To communicate with you:** confirmations, receipts, reminders, schedule changes and answers to your requests. These are service messages, not marketing.
5. **To improve the website:** understanding which pages work, fixing errors and keeping the site secure.
6. **To meet legal duties:** accounting, tax, responding to lawful requests and protecting our rights.

**Legal basis.** We process personal data on the basis of your **consent**, or for **legitimate uses** permitted by law. A legitimate use includes data you voluntarily give us for a specific purpose, such as registering for an event. We ask only for data that is needed for the purpose stated at the point of collection.

## 5. Marketing is always optional

- We send newsletters or promotional messages **only if you separately opt in**. The box is never pre-ticked.
- Registering for an event or using our website **never requires** agreeing to marketing.
- You can withdraw marketing consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link or emailing us. Withdrawing it does not affect your registration.

## 6. Children and young people

Our community welcomes young founders, and some participants may be **under 18**. Indian law treats them as children.

- Where we knowingly process the personal data of a child, we seek **verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian**, for example through a guardian consent form for event registration or Pitch Arena applications.
- We do **not** track, behaviourally monitor or show targeted advertising to children.
- A parent or guardian can contact us at any time to review, correct or withdraw a child's data.

## 7. Who we share data with

We **do not sell** your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run Flareonix, and only to the extent needed:

| Provider type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Website hosting, database and sign-in (Emergent, MongoDB, Google) | Running the website and secure sign-in |
| Email delivery (Resend, via Emergent) | Sending confirmations, receipts and password-reset emails |
| Analytics (Google Analytics, PostHog) | Understanding how the site is used; PostHog may record anonymised session replays |
| AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI) | Generating AI Tools results from the prompts you submit |
| Payment provider or bank (when payments are enabled) | Processing and verifying payments |
| Messaging (WhatsApp, Instagram) | Only when you choose to contact us there |

**Sponsors and partners do not receive attendee lists** or contact details unless you give separate, explicit consent for that specific sharing.

We may disclose data where required by law, court order or a lawful government request, or to protect the safety of people at our events.

## 8. Data stored outside India

Some of our service providers store or process data outside India. Where this happens we rely on transfers permitted under Indian law and choose providers that use appropriate security safeguards.

## 9. How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account details | While your account is active, and up to 12 months after your last sign-in |
| Enquiries and consultation forms | Up to 24 months after our last interaction |
| Event registrations, payments and refunds | As long as required by tax and accounting laws |
| Check-in records | Up to 12 months after the event |
| Pitch Arena applications and decks | Deleted within 12 months after the event unless you agree otherwise |
| Comments | Until you ask us to remove them or we remove them under our Terms |
| AI Tools history | Until you delete it from your dashboard or your account is removed |
| Analytics data | According to the provider's retention settings |

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

## 10. How we protect data

We use reasonable security safeguards, including encrypted HTTPS connections, access limited to the people who need it, hashed admin passwords, short-lived admin sessions, sign-in attempt limits and activity logs. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected people and the authorities as required by law.

## 11. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a **summary** of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
- **correct, complete or update** inaccurate data;
- **erase** data that is no longer needed, unless we must keep it by law;
- **withdraw consent** you have given (this does not affect processing before withdrawal);
- **nominate** a person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapable;
- resolve a **grievance** (see our [Grievance Redressal](/policies/grievance) page).

To make a request, email [hello@flareonix.in](mailto:hello@flareonix.in) with the subject **"Privacy request"**. We may need to verify your identity before sharing or changing records. We will respond within the time limits required by law.

## 12. Cookies and analytics

- **Essential cookies** keep you signed in (for example `session_token`) and protect the admin area. The website cannot work properly without them.
- **Analytics cookies and tools** (Google Analytics, PostHog) help us understand visits and fix problems.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and use the opt-out tools offered by Google Analytics. Blocking essential cookies may stop sign-in from working.
