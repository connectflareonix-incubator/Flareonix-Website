The Pitch Arena gives early-stage founders a stage to present their startup at Flareonix events. These rules apply to every application and pitch. Event pages show the deadlines, format, time limits and any awards for each event.

**FFT #001:** applications close on **5 December 2026**.

## 1. Who can apply

- Founders or co-founders of an early-stage startup or idea. The event page may set stage, age or location criteria.
- Founders **under 18** need **parent or guardian consent** to apply and to pitch.
- Each startup should apply once per event. The information you give must be accurate and your own.

## 2. Your idea stays yours

- **You keep all intellectual property** in your startup, pitch deck, product and ideas.
- By applying, you give Flareonix a **limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free permission** to store your application and share it **only with the review panel**, for evaluating it.
- If you are selected, you also allow us to show your startup name, logo and a short description in event materials. You can ask us to stop at any time.
- Flareonix will not use your idea to build a competing product, or share your deck publicly, without your written permission.

## 3. Confidentiality: please read

- We limit access to your application to the people reviewing it, and take reasonable care to keep it safe.
- A pitch made on stage in front of an audience is **not confidential**. Do not include trade secrets, unfiled patent details or anything you are not comfortable sharing publicly.
- We cannot guarantee that judges, attendees or investors will not be working on similar ideas. **No NDA applies** unless one is signed separately.

## 4. Review and selection

- Applications are reviewed by a Flareonix panel against criteria such as problem clarity, solution, market, traction, team and pitch readiness.
- Selection decisions are final. Because of volume, we may not be able to give individual feedback.
- Selected founders receive a **complimentary, named, non-transferable pass** where the event page says so.

## 5. No guarantees

Being selected, pitching or winning **does not guarantee** investment, funding, mentorship, partnerships, incubation, media coverage or any other result. Investors and guests take part independently, and any deal is solely between you and them.

## 6. Awards and recognition

Awards and recognition are announced on the event page. Winners may need to share ID and payment details to receive a prize. Taxes apply as required by law. We may withhold an award if the rules are broken.

## 7. Disqualification

We may reject or disqualify an application or pitch that contains false information or plagiarism, breaks the law, infringes someone else's rights, or breaks the Event Participation Terms.

## 8. Your data and your deck

- Pitch decks are stored privately and accessed only by reviewers.
- We delete decks and application details **within 12 months after the event**, unless you are selected and agree to a longer period.
- You can withdraw your application before the deadline by emailing [hello@flareonix.in](mailto:hello@flareonix.in).
