This policy applies to **paid passes** for Flareonix events, including the Flareonix Founders Table. Event pages may state additional details for a specific event, and those details apply for that event.

## 1. If Flareonix cancels an event

You will receive a **full refund of the pass price** to your original payment method, or to the account you paid from for UPI or bank transfers.

## 2. If an event is postponed or moved

- Your pass stays valid for the new date or venue.
- If you cannot attend the new date, you can ask for a full refund **within 7 days** of our postponement notice.

## 3. If you cancel

| When you ask | What happens |
|---|---|
| **7 days or more** before the event starts | Refund of the pass price, minus any non-refundable payment processing charges shown at checkout |
| **Less than 7 days** before the event starts | No refund, but you may transfer your pass (see section 4) |
| After the event starts, or if you do not attend | No refund |

## 4. Transferring your pass

You may transfer your pass to another person **up to 48 hours before the event** by emailing us with your pass ID and the new attendee's name and email. The new attendee must accept the Event Participation Terms and, if under 18, provide guardian consent. We will reissue the pass in their name, and the original pass will stop working.

## 5. Passes that cannot be refunded

- Complimentary or allocated passes (for example for Pitch Arena founders, speakers or partners).
- Passes cancelled because the holder broke the Event Participation Terms or Code of Conduct.
- Passes obtained through fraud, chargebacks or false information.

## 6. How to request a refund or transfer

Email [hello@flareonix.in](mailto:hello@flareonix.in) with the subject **"Refund request – [event name] – [pass ID]"**, and include:

- your name and the email used at registration;
- your pass ID;
- for UPI or bank payments, the transaction reference (UTR) and the account to refund.

We may verify your identity before making a refund. **Never send us your UPI PIN, card details or banking password.**

## 7. Timelines

- We aim to acknowledge refund requests **within 48 hours**.
- Approved refunds are initiated **within 7 working days**. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to show the credit.

## 8. Payment disputes and chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank, because most issues can be resolved quickly. If a payment is reversed, the related pass is cancelled.
