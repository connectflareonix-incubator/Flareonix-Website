## 2. What you can raise

- Privacy and personal data: access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or nominating a person.
- Payments, refunds, pass transfers and failed transactions.
- Event experience, conduct or safety concerns.
- Pitch Arena applications and decisions.
- Comments or content on the website that you believe is unlawful or harmful.
- Accessibility problems.

## 3. How to raise a grievance

Email [hello@flareonix.in](mailto:hello@flareonix.in) with:

- a clear subject, for example "Grievance – Refund" or "Privacy request";
- your name and the email you used with us;
- any pass ID, application ID or link that helps us find the record;
- what happened and what outcome you are looking for.

**Never send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or full card details.** To protect your privacy, we may verify your identity before we share or change any records.

## 4. Our response

- We aim to **acknowledge** your grievance **within 48 hours**.
- We aim to **resolve** it **within 30 days**, and will keep you updated if it needs more time.
- Reports about harmful content on our website are reviewed as quickly as possible, and content may be hidden while we investigate.

## 5. If you are not satisfied

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach:

- the **Data Protection Board of India**, for personal data concerns, as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023;
- the **National Consumer Helpline** (1915, or [consumerhelpline.gov.in](https://consumerhelpline.gov.in)), or the appropriate consumer commission, for consumer issues.
