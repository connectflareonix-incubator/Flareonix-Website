Flareonix events are photographed and filmed so we can document the community and share it with others. This policy explains how we use photos and videos, and the choices you have.

## 1. What we capture and why

We may take photos, videos and audio recordings of sessions, the Pitch Arena, award moments, networking and the venue. We use them:

- on the Flareonix website, event galleries and social media;
- in event recaps, reports, press and promotional material for future Flareonix events;
- to keep a record of the event.

**We do not sell photos of attendees.**

## 2. Opting out

- Tell the registration desk or any Flareonix team member that you do not want to be photographed. Where possible, we will give you a visible marker, such as a coloured badge, and our photographers will avoid featuring you.
- Crowd and wide shots may still include you in the background.

## 3. Asking us to remove an image

Email [hello@flareonix.in](mailto:hello@flareonix.in) with the image or link and a short description. We aim to remove it from Flareonix-controlled channels within **7 working days**. We cannot remove copies that others have already shared or printed, but we will help where we reasonably can.

## 4. Children

We do **not** feature identifiable images of attendees **under 18** in promotional material without the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 5. Speakers, judges and pitching founders

Talks, panels and pitches may be recorded and published. Speakers and selected founders will be told in advance and can raise concerns before the event. Slides or content marked confidential will not be published.

## 6. Your own photos and recordings

You may take photos for personal use. Please respect others who do not wish to be photographed, and do not record sessions announced as private.

## 7. Ownership

Photos and videos taken by Flareonix or its appointed photographers belong to Flareonix. Your rights over your personal data are described in our [Privacy Policy](/policies/privacy).
