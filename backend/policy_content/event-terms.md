## 1. Passes

- You need a **valid pass** to enter. A pass is issued only after **verified payment** or an **approved complimentary allocation** (for example for selected Pitch Arena founders, speakers or partners).
- Each pass admits **one person once**. The QR code or pass ID can be scanned only once, and copies or screenshots of a used code will be refused.
- Passes are **personal**. They may be transferred only as allowed in the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](/policies/refunds).
- Passes bought from unofficial sellers, or obtained through fraud, chargebacks or false information, are invalid.

## 2. Prices and charges

The pass price, what it includes, and whether taxes and payment charges are included are shown **before you pay**. Early-bird prices apply only during the stated sale period or until the early-bird allocation runs out.

## 3. Age and guardian consent

- Unless the event page says otherwise, attendees **under 18** must submit a **parent or guardian consent** before the event, and may be asked to attend with an adult.
- We may ask for proof of age at entry.

## 4. Entry and identity

- Bring your pass and a valid photo ID. The name on your pass should match your ID.
- Arrive on time, because late entry may be limited during talks or the Pitch Arena.
- We may refuse entry or ask a person to leave for safety reasons, for breaking this Code of Conduct or the venue's rules, or if their pass is invalid.

## 5. Code of conduct

Flareonix events are built on respect and ambition. Everyone attending (attendees, speakers, founders, sponsors, volunteers and staff) must:

- treat others with respect, whatever their gender, caste, religion, region, language, disability, age or background;
- avoid harassment, bullying, intimidation, unwanted contact or unwelcome photography;
- not arrive intoxicated or bring illegal substances, weapons or dangerous items;
- follow the instructions of event staff and venue security;
- respect speakers, pitching founders and the agreed timing.

To report a concern, speak to any Flareonix team member at the event or email [hello@flareonix.in](mailto:hello@flareonix.in). We take every report seriously and handle it discreetly.
